# Linkcardly product (decided 2026-10-17)

Positioning: **"Your business. One tap."** A digital business profile, with an NFC card as the physical way to open it. Lead with the profile, not the technology.

## Products (one-time prices, no yearly plans)
| Product | India | USA | What's in it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Digital Card | ₹799 | $29 | Profile, custom link, QR code, unlimited edits. Never expires. |
| NFC Card: Basic | ₹1,499 + ₹99 shipping | $59 | Digital Card + NFC card in one of 3 standard designs, programmed to the profile. |
| NFC Card: Custom | ₹2,499 + ₹99 shipping | $89 | Basic + the customer's logo and colours on one of 3 layouts (Modern, Luxury, Minimal). |

- Motion Card: **disabled**, not deleted. Hidden from the site and the order flow; code and data stay.
- Existing yearly cards become lifetime (no renewal reminders, no expiry).
- NFC ships within India only (₹99). US NFC orders: later.
- No GST yet (not registered). Prices are final amounts.

## Custom NFC card (self-serve)
Customer uploads a logo → the page pulls its colours → live preview on the 3 layouts → customer picks and approves → admin only checks the print file. No design work by hand, no WhatsApp back-and-forth.

## Checkout for NFC
Full address, PIN code, delivery phone (if different from the card's phone). Paid fully online (Razorpay, once approved).

## Partners
₹200 commission per paid order (any product). Customers get ₹100 off any product with a partner code.

## Site structure
Header: **Digital Card · NFC Cards** (submenu: Basic, Custom) **· Pricing**. Teams stays in the footer.
- Home answers "What do you need?" with three options: Digital Card, NFC Basic, NFC Custom.
- `/nfc-cards`: Basic and Custom side by side, each with an order button.
- All designs stay available (no cut to 5).

## Later (after the first 30 paying customers)
**Business Pro**, an optional yearly add-on, price to be decided: views, taps and QR scans per card; team dashboard comparing agents; monthly report by email; no Linkcardly branding; update all team cards at once.

## Goal
30 real paying customers. Validate in person (realtors, salons, photographers, insurance, trainers) with 3–4 samples.

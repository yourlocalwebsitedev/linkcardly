FAQ

Quick answers.

Can I change my card after I order?

Yes. You get a private edit link by email. Update your details or switch to another design any time, free.

What happens when my year is up?

We email you before your renewal date. Renew to keep your link and QR code working. If you need a little time, your card stays live for 15 days after the renewal date.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. Ask within 14 days of paying, for any reason, and we refund you in full.

Who can see my details?

Everything on a live card can be seen by anyone with the link, so only add what you're happy to share. We don't sell personal data or use it for advertising.

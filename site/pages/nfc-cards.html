FAQ

NFC card questions.

Is the token amount extra?

No. It comes off the price of your order, and you pay the rest when you get your stand.

Which phones can read the card?

Most iPhones from the XR on and most Android phones with NFC switched on. Phones without NFC can scan the QR code on the back.

Can I change where the card points?

Yes. Ask us and we point it to a new link.

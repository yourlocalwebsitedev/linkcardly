FAQ

Pricing questions.

What happens when my year is up?

We email you before your renewal date. Renew to keep your link and QR code working. If you need a little time, your card stays live for 15 days after the renewal date.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. Ask within 14 days of paying, for any reason, and we refund you in full.

How do I pay?

Checkout runs on Stripe in the US and Razorpay in India. We never see or store your card number.

What is the Motion Card?

It adds a moving portrait to the top of your card: a short selfie video or GIF, up to 10 seconds. Everything else is the same as the Digital Card.

Do you do cards for a whole team?

Yes, for teams of 20 or more. Send us a quote request and we reply within one business day with pricing and a sample card.

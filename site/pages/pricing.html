FAQ

Pricing questions.

Do I pay every year?

No. You pay once and your card stays live, with your link and QR code. Edits and restyles are free, any time.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. Ask within 14 days of paying, for any reason, and we refund you in full.

How do I pay?

Checkout runs on Stripe in the US and Razorpay in India. We never see or store your card number.

What's the difference between NFC Basic and Custom?

Basic is one of our three standard card designs. Custom puts your logo and brand colours on the card: you upload your logo, see a live preview and approve it before we print.

Do you do cards for a whole team?

Yes, for teams of 20 or more. Send us a quote request and we reply within one business day with pricing and a sample card.

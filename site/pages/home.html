FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do people need an app to see my card?

No. Your card is a web page, so it opens in the browser on any phone or computer when someone taps your link, scans your QR code or taps your NFC card.

How do people save my details?

They tap Save contact. Your name, number, email, company and photo go straight into their phone contacts.

Can I change my card after I order?

Yes. You get a private edit link by email. Update your details or switch to another design any time, free.

What happens when my year is up?

We email you before your renewal date. Renew to keep your link and QR code working. If you need a little time, your card stays live for 15 days after the renewal date.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. Ask within 14 days of paying, for any reason, and we refund you in full.

How do I pay?

Checkout runs on Stripe in the US and Razorpay in India. We never see or store your card number.

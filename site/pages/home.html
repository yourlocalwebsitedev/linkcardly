Good questions

Do they need an app?

No. Your card opens from a QR scan or a link in any phone browser.

Can I edit after sharing?

Any time, from your private edit link. Your link and QR keep working.

Can people book or message me?

Yes. Add a booking button for your calendar, and turn on the lead form to collect enquiries.

What if my name is taken?

Type it above and we'll suggest a free variation, like your business name or a middle initial.

Can I switch designs later?

Yes. Change family or colourway as often as you like without retyping your details.

Does it work for teams?

Yes. Approve, pause and manage every card and lead from one admin panel. See Teams.

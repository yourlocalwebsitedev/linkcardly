FAQ

About your link.

Will my link ever change?

No. linkcardly.com/yourname stays the same for good, even when you change your design or details.

Do people need an app to see my card?

No. Your card is a web page, so it opens in the browser on any phone or computer when someone taps your link, scans your QR code or taps your NFC card.

What happens when my year is up?

We email you before your renewal date. Renew to keep your link and QR code working. If you need a little time, your card stays live for 15 days after the renewal date.
